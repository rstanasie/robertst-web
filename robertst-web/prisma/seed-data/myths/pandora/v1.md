## Made to Order {#made-to-order}

After the fire was stolen, Zeus did not take it back. He answered it instead. He
had her made — clay again, the same material, by the same forge — and every god
brought something to the finishing.

Grace. Skill at the loom. A voice. And curiosity, given last, and given
deliberately.

## The Gift {#the-gift}

She was sent down with a jar and told not to open it. Not warned about what was
in it. Told not to open it, and given the curiosity that made not-opening
impossible, by the being who had arranged both.

It is worth being precise about who set this up.

## What Came Out {#what-came-out}

Everything that troubles a life. Illness, labour, grief, the knowledge of an
ending. They went out over the world in an afternoon and have not been gathered
back.

## What Stayed {#what-stayed}

One thing was still in the jar when she got the lid down: elpis, which we
translate as hope and which might as easily be translated as expectation — the
habit of believing tomorrow owes you something.

Whether that counts as a mercy or the last item of the punishment is not settled,
and the Greeks did not think it was.
