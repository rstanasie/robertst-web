## Three Sisters {#three-sisters}

Two of them could not die. Stheno and Euryale were born to the sea's old powers
and would outlast the coastline. The third was born mortal, into the same house,
with the same face, and had to live alongside that difference every day of her
life.

## The Temple {#the-temple}

What happened in Athena's temple is told two ways, and the difference between
them is the whole story. In one she is a trespasser. In the other she is a girl
who ran to a goddess for protection and was given, instead, a punishment for
having been caught in the wrong place.

Athena did not curse the god who followed her in. She cursed the hair.

## The Cave {#the-cave}

So she went where no one would look at her, and no one did, and that was the
mercy in it. The garden of statues around her cave was not a trophy hall. It was
a record of every person who had come anyway.

## The Mirror {#the-mirror}

Perseus arrived with a polished shield and did not look at her once. He watched
her reflection while he worked. It is worth noticing that the hero's great
cleverness was a way of not having to see her face.

Afterwards they set the head on a shield, and she went on doing the only thing
she had ever been able to do — to anyone who looked.
