## The Voice {#the-voice}

Stones moved for him. This is stated flatly in every source, without wonder, the
way you would mention that a man was tall. Rivers turned to hear him. Trees came
downhill and stayed.

It made him careless about what could be refused.

## The Descent {#the-descent}

Eurydice died on her wedding day, of a snake in the long grass, and he went down
after her — not by any secret door, but by the ordinary road, singing.

The ferryman took him without payment. The dog lay down. Sisyphus sat on his
stone and listened, and for the length of one song nothing in that country was
punished.

## The Condition {#the-condition}

Take her, said the queen of the dead. Walk ahead of her. Do not look back until
you are both in the light.

He agreed instantly, the way people do.

## The Turn {#the-turn}

The dead make no sound when they walk. He had a whole ascent in which to wonder
whether she was behind him at all, and no way to check that was not the one
forbidden thing.

At the mouth of the cave, with the light on his own hands, he turned. She was
there. That is the cruelty of it — she was there, and had been the entire way.
